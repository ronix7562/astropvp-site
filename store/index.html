    Payment info

    Checkout will be handled by Tebex, our payment provider. Tebex processes the payment and keeps the order records; AstroPvP never sees your card details. The store is not open yet: buttons show Coming soon until checkout is ready.

    Questions about an order? Open a ticket in our Discord and include your in-game name and the order or transaction ID from your Tebex receipt.

    Refunds

    • All purchases are final, except where the law or Tebex's terms give you a right to a refund.
    • Chargebacks or fraudulent payments may lead to a punishment or a permanent ban.
    • If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy.

    Info
