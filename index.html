Server Rules

Playing on AstroPvP means you accept these rules. Staff may punish anything that harms the server or its players, even if it isn't listed. Pick a category below.

General Chat Factions & PvP Economy & Trading Staff & Punishments

General

Fair play for everyone, on every server of the network.

  1. No cheating. Hacked clients, killaura, reach, fly, x-ray (mods or texture packs), auto-clickers, macros and any client that gives an unfair advantage are banned. Allowed: Cosmic Client, Lunar, Badlion, OptiFine and vanilla 1.8.9.
  2. No exploiting bugs. Duplication, glitching through blocks or claims, and abusing broken plugins are forbidden. Report a bug to staff on Discord. Players who report real exploits can be rewarded.
  3. Alts. Alt accounts are allowed, but they can't be used to dodge a punishment, farm events, vote twice, boost a faction's power or get around faction limits.
  4. No real-money trading. Selling or buying accounts, items, money or factions for real money or for things outside AstroPvP is not allowed. The only real-money purchases are on our official store.
  5. No AFK farming machines. Auto-fishing, AFK mob farms with macros and anything that plays the game for you are not allowed.
  6. Account safety is yours. You are responsible for everything done on your account. Never share your password or your /pin. Staff will never ask for them.
  7. No impersonation. Don't pretend to be staff, a YouTuber or another player, in game or on Discord.
  8. Don't evade punishments. Joining on another account while banned or muted makes the punishment longer, and the alt is banned too.

Direct link: #rules-general

Chat

Public chat, private messages, mail, signs, books and item names all count.

  1. Respect everyone. No harassment, threats, racism, sexism, homophobia or hate toward any group. Trash talk about the game is fine. Attacking someone as a person is not.
  2. No spam. Don't repeat messages, flood chat, type in all caps or send many messages in a row.
  3. No advertising. Don't name or link other servers, Discords or stores. Promoting your own YouTube or Twitch is allowed only in the media channels on our Discord.
  4. Links. Only links to AstroPvP pages, our Discord and screenshots (imgur, Gyazo, Lightshot) are allowed. Never post download links, IP grabbers or scam links.
  5. Language. Keep chat in English so staff can read it. Light swearing is tolerated. Heavy or targeted swearing and slurs are punished.
  6. No doxxing or threats. Sharing someone's real name, address, photos or other private info, or threatening to DDoS or hack them, means a permanent ban.
  7. No inappropriate content. No sexual content, gore or drug talk in chat, names, skins, banners or builds.
  8. Don't fake punishments or announcements. Messages made to look like a ban, a broadcast or a staff message are not allowed.

Direct link: #rules-chat

Factions & PvP

Raiding and fighting are the point of the server. These rules keep it fair.

  1. Raiding is allowed. TNT, creepers and cannons against a raidable faction are part of the game, as is taking whatever you find inside.
  2. Griefing claims. Unclaimed land is free for all. Inside someone's claim you may only damage what the game lets you (raids, explosions). Don't use bugs to break, place or open things in a claim.
  3. Traps are allowed, with one exception. Combat traps, TP traps and lure traps are fair. Traps that kill or trap players at spawn, /warp locations or outposts' safe platforms are not.
  4. Ally and truce limits. Stay inside the faction member, ally and truce limits. Teaming with other factions in fights or raids to get around those limits is not allowed.
  5. No combat logging. Logging out, disconnecting or using /logout while tagged kills you and drops your items. Doing it on purpose to avoid a fight can also be punished.
  6. No safe-zone camping abuse. Don't hit players from spawn or another safe zone to get free hits. You can't attack back from there.
  7. Faction names and banners must follow the chat rules. Offensive ones are disbanded or renamed.
  8. Leaving a faction. Taking items with you is part of the game. Using a faction's claims, power or chests through a bug is not.
  9. Events and KoTH. No cross-teaming during events, KoTHs, outposts or envoys unless the event allows it.

Direct link: #rules-factions

Economy & Trading

Use /trade or the auction house. Staff can't return items lost in a deal you made yourself.

  1. No scamming. Taking someone's items or money and not giving what you promised is punished, whether in /trade, a drop trade, coinflip or the auction house.
  2. No fake trades. Renamed or fake items, swapping items at the last second and lying about an item's enchants or set are scams.
  3. Gift cards and Tebex codes. Gift card codes are worth real money. Never share them in public, never trade them for in-game items and never sell them. A code given away or traded is not refunded.
  4. Store purchases. Ranks, keys and kits bought on the store can't be traded for real money. Chargebacks and disputes mean a permanent ban on every account.
  5. No market manipulation with alts. Moving money or items between your own accounts to farm rewards, leaderboards or /baltop is not allowed.
  6. Gambling games (coinflip, bombs, mines) are your own risk. Lost bets are not refunded.
  7. Borrowing items or money is at your own risk. Staff don't handle unpaid loans.

Direct link: #rules-economy

Staff & Punishments

Staff decide based on what happened and your history. Repeating a rule break moves you up the ladder.

  1. Follow staff instructions. If a staff member freezes you or asks for a screenshare, stay online and do what they ask. Logging out while frozen is a ban.
  2. Don't argue in public chat. If you disagree with a punishment, appeal on Discord instead.
  3. Report players properly. Use /report in game, or a ticket on Discord with a video or screenshots. False reports are punished.
  4. Appeals. Open a ticket in our Discord (discord.gg/NtYpkCzpMy). Say your IGN, the punishment, and why it should be removed or shortened. Be honest: lying in an appeal means it is denied.
  5. One appeal per punishment every 7 days. Spamming staff about it does not help.
  • WarnA written warning in chat. It stays on your record.
  • MuteYou can't chat, message or mail anyone for a set time. Used for chat rules.
  • TempbanYou can't join the network for hours or days. Used for repeated or serious breaks.
  • BanPermanent removal from the network. You can appeal on Discord.
  • BlacklistPermanent ban on every account and IP, with no appeal. Used for chargebacks, doxxing, DDoS threats and selling cheats.
